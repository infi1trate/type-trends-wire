- The desks' rules stay pure in `copy.ts` (lints), `standards.ts` (checklist, second-reader prompt and parser), `chief.ts` (placement prompt and after-call enforcement) and `review.ts` (order of work, caps, fallbacks); `review.server.ts` only loads, calls the gateway and records, and every desk decision goes through the door as its desk, so tests replay the whole pass with fake I/O.
- A story the engine would run is stored held awaiting review and published only by the editor in chief's (or the owner's) `restore` after a standards pass newer than its last text or correction; a failed or capped desk call falls back to engine order with the reason recorded, so a failing desk never stops the day's stories.
- Desk prompts are versioned in `golden/versions.ts` with score files from frozen fixtures; the gate compares cases both versions ran, because a fixture re-run is limited by the model-call budget.
- News, graphics and data desks keep their rules pure in `news.ts`, `graphics.ts` and `datadesk.ts` (candidates, line checks, picture decisions, findings, backtest); `*.server.ts` only load, make the one capped call (`caps.ts`) and record through the door, and a failed or capped call leaves the rule's result standing with the reason recorded.
- The product loop's rules stay pure in `design-review.ts` (pages, widths, measures, budgets, breaches, the vision prompt and move enforcement), `queue.ts` (statuses and the order), `build-gate.ts` (the gate's checks, calling the living-design `gate()`) and `engineering.ts` (health problems by stable key); `*.server.ts` only load, store append-only rows and file through the door, so the outside browser job and engineering sessions are held to the same rules as tests.
